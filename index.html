    About ImageCombiner Web

    ImageCombiner Web is a fast, private, and simple tool to arrange multiple photos and animated GIFs into a clean collage grid right inside your browser.

    1

    Privacy and Terms

    100% In-Browser & Local — No Server Involved

    ImageCombiner Web runs entirely inside your web browser. There is no server, no cloud storage, and no tracking. Your photos and animations never leave your computer or phone. All image cropping, combining, and exporting happen directly in your device's local memory.

    License & Terms: This project is free, open source software licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0). You are completely free to use it, modify it, and share it.

    2

    Features

    Mix Photos & Animated GIFs

    Combine static pictures (PNG, JPG, WebP, SVG, BMP) and animated GIFs together in the exact same collage grid.

    Text Overlays & Captions

    Add top, center, or bottom text captions. Use // to split different captions across individual photos (e.g. Before // After), with custom fonts and sizes.

    Live Animation Controls & Sync

    Play, pause, scrub frames in real time, adjust playback speed (0.5x, 1x, 2x), and set start delays to synchronize animations.

    Smart & Custom Grid Layouts

    Smart layout algorithm balances rows and columns so images stay close to square, or choose custom layouts (1 to 4 columns, or a single row).

    Interactive Pan, Zoom & Fit

    Drag photos directly on the canvas to pan, zoom with Shift + scroll wheel or sliders, and fit cells cleanly without distortion.

    Flexible Borders & Spacing

    Independently adjust outer frame borders, inner spacing gaps between photos, and background color (including transparent).

    Preset & Custom Aspect Ratios

    Choose popular aspect ratios (1:1 Square, 16:9 Landscape, 9:16 Vertical Story, 4:3, etc.) or set exact pixel dimensions.

    Crisp High-Resolution Export

    Save finished collages in high resolution up to 16+ megapixels as PNG, JPG, WebP, animated GIF, or animated WebP.
