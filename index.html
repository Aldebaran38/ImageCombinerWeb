    About ImageCombiner Web

    ImageCombiner Web is a private, lightweight, and fast tool that allows you to arrange multiple photos and animated GIFs into a single, clean collage right in your web browser.

    100% Private & Client-Side

    Your photos never leave your device. All calculations, cropping, and rendering happen right in your browser's local memory. No servers, no tracking, and no cloud uploads.

    Full Animated GIF & WebP Support

    Mix static photos with animated GIFs seamlessly. Preview animations live with scrub and speed controls, adjust start-time alignment, and export as animated GIF or WebP.

    Smart Grid Balance

    The layout engine automatically evaluates row and column combinations to find the arrangement where individual photos look closest to square and balanced.

    Center-Crop Fitting

    Photos fill each cell completely from the center without stretching or introducing unwanted black bars.

    Flexible Borders & Spacing

    Customize outer border frames and inner gaps between photos independently, or remove borders completely with full color control.

    Crisp High-Resolution Export

    Export in PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WEBP up to 16+ Megapixels for print quality, social media posts, or portfolios.

    Advanced Animation Timing

    Set a start time offset for each animation to sync them up or stagger their motions.
